# Wallpaper credits

All three pictures in `wallpapers/` are drawn from code by `make_wallpapers.py` for this theme by
Christopher Hrehor and released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), not MIT. Each is a near-black
background (#0A0606) with two soft glows, faint diagonal rain streaks and fine scanlines; the rain and
scanlines are softened behind the clock and the dock so text stays easy to read. Saved as JPEG without
chroma subsampling (4:4:4).

- `morning.jpg`: ember and gold glows, sunrise to noon. CC0 1.0.
- `evening.jpg`: crimson and gold glows, noon until half an hour after sunset. CC0 1.0.
- `night.jpg`: midnight blue and crimson glows, the rest of the night. CC0 1.0.
